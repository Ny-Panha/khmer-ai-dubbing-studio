# Spec: Timeline UI, Features & Workflow Fix

## 1. Problem & Intent (Why)
- **Context & Background**: Khmer AI Dubbing Studio's bottom multi-track timeline (`TimelineEditor.jsx`, `TimelineToolbar.jsx`, `TimelineTrackHeaders.jsx`, `TimelineTracksCanvas.jsx`, `TimelineEditor.css`) had visual and functional issues identified in studio screenshots.
- **Problem Statement**:
  1. **`+ + ឃ្លា` Duplicate Plus Bug (`TimelineToolbar.jsx`)**: Renders `<Plus size={12} />` next to literal `"+ ឃ្លា"`, producing `+ + ឃ្លា`.
  2. **Messy Toolbar Flow (`TimelineToolbar.jsx`)**: Actions are mixed without clear dubbing workflow hierarchy or center live timecode readout.
  3. **Desynced Mute Controls & Truncated `CLEAN B` Header (`TimelineTrackHeaders.jsx` & `TimelineToolbar.jsx`)**: `.track-headers-column` (`112px`) cuts off `CLEAN BGM` as `CLEAN B`, and clicking Mute on `VIDEO 1` or `CLEAN BGM` track headers is disconnected from `onToggleMuteAllCuts` and `voiceSettings.duckingVolume`.
  4. **Empty `VOICE 2` Track & Cramped `#...` Clips (`TimelineTracksCanvas.jsx` & `TimelineEditor.jsx`)**: All segments default to `VOICE 1` (`row: 0`) unless colliding, leaving `VOICE 2` empty even when Male (`ប្រុស`) and Female (`ស្រី`) speakers alternate or back-to-back clips (`gap < 0.15s`) crowd `VOICE 1`. Narrow clips (`< 48px`) truncate labels into `#...`.
  5. **Playhead Blends into Selected Clip (`TimelineEditor.css`)**: Both `.timeline-playhead` / `.playhead-line` and `.selected` clip borders use cyan (`#38BDF8` / `#00e5ff`), making the playhead stem blend into selected clips.

## 2. User Scenarios & Stories (What)
- **Scenario 1 (Clean 3-Section Toolbar & `ថែមឃ្លា` Button)**: User sees Left Workflow Actions (1. History & Clip Edit: `Undo`, `Redo`, `Split`, `Delete`, `<Plus size={13} /> <span>ថែមឃ្លា</span>` -> 2. Video Slicing Mode segmented control: `Full Video | Auto-Cut` -> 3. Voice Timing & Alignment: `Auto-Align`, `Bridge Gaps`, `Reset Timing`), Center live monospace Timecode (`MM:SS / MM:SS`), and Right Toggles & Zoom (`Snap`, `Subs`, `Zoom Out/Slider/In`, `Fit`, `Auto-Center`).
- **Scenario 2 (`148px` Non-Truncated Track Headers & Two-Way Mute Sync)**: User sees full `VIDEO 1`, `VOICE 1 (👨/គោល)`, `VOICE 2 (👩/បន្ទាប់)`, `SUBTITLES`, and `CLEAN BGM` labels with colored `3px` left status bars and `22px x 22px` `Mute`/`Solo`/`Lock` buttons. Clicking Mute on `VIDEO 1` triggers `onToggleMuteAllCuts(true)` and dims the lane (`opacity: 0.45`) with a `MUTED` badge; clicking Mute on `CLEAN BGM` updates `voiceSettings.duckingVolume` (`0` vs `0.15`) and dims the lane with a `MUTED` badge.
- **Scenario 3 (Smart `VOICE 1` / `VOICE 2` Routing & Responsive Clip Labels)**: Unshifted segments automatically route Male (`ប្រុស`) to `VOICE 1` (`row: 0`) and Female (`ស្រី`) to `VOICE 2` (`row: 1`), or stagger overlapping / tight back-to-back clips (`gap < 0.15s`) onto `VOICE 2` when single-gender. Voice clips use deep studio blue (`oklch(0.28 0.08 250)`) for Male and deep studio rose (`oklch(0.28 0.09 350)`) for Female, render `#N` only when `< 48px`, `#N ប្រុស/ស្រី` when `48px..94px`, and `#N ប្រុស/ស្រី` + 1-line Khmer dialogue preview (`Kantumruy Pro` `11px`) when `>= 95px`, with a full multi-line tooltip on hover.
- **Scenario 4 (High-Contrast Coral Red Playhead)**: Playhead scrubber stem and handle use CapCut Coral Red (`#ff3b5c`) with subtle glow (`0 0 6px rgba(255, 59, 92, 0.5)`) and `z-index: 60`, while `#00e5ff` / `#38BDF8` stays reserved for `.selected` clip outlines and trim handles.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `TimelineToolbar.jsx` renders `<Plus size={13} />` and `<span>ថែមឃ្លា</span>` (zero `+ + ឃ្លា`), exposes `onBridgeGaps`, groups `Full Video` and `Auto-Cut` into a segmented pill toggle, displays center timecode `<span className="timeline-timecode-current">{formatMinSec(currentTime)}</span> / <span className="timeline-timecode-total">{formatMinSec(duration)}</span>`, and supports zoom slider `0.5` to `3.0`.
- [ ] `TimelineTrackHeaders.jsx` and `TimelineEditor.css` set `.track-headers-column` to `148px` (`TRACK_HEADER_WIDTH = 148`), display `3px` colored left status bars, `VOICE 1` (`👨/គោល`) and `VOICE 2` (`👩/បន្ទាប់`) sub-badges, `22px x 22px` control buttons, and two-way bind `VIDEO 1` mute (`onToggleMuteAllCuts`) and `CLEAN BGM` mute (`voiceSettings.duckingVolume`).
- [ ] `TimelineTracksCanvas.jsx` dims muted lanes (`opacity: 0.45`, grayscale waveform) and shows a `MUTED` badge, routes unshifted Male/Female or tight back-to-back (`gap < 0.15s`) segments across `VOICE 1` and `VOICE 2`, and renders responsive clip labels (`< 48px`, `48px..94px`, `>= 95px`) with full Khmer dialogue tooltip.
- [ ] `TimelineEditor.css` styles `.timeline-playhead`, `.playhead-line`, `.capcut-playhead-line`, and `.playhead-handle` in `#ff3b5c` with `0 0 6px rgba(255, 59, 92, 0.5)` glow and `z-index: 60`.
- [ ] All existing regression tests in `backend/tests/timeline_*.test.js` and new verification tests pass 100%.
